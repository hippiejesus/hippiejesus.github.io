Obsession

Curry Barker

★★★★★

June 7, 2026

An instant-classic thriller about having someone's autonomy completely ripped from them and forced into a situation they have no way of escaping. First "horror" movie in a while that felt like a movie first instead of a series of shit that's supposed to spook you.

Give this one a watch.
