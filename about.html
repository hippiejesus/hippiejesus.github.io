About

Greetings! I'm Dean, and welcome to my personal online landfill.

I don't have a plan for many of the things that are included on this website, so expect massive changes out of the blue. I try to include new things to do and more content, so check by often.

Photography, videography, and (listening to) music is what I spend most of my free time doing. Please, check out my gallery!

I've been carrying a camera with me everywhere I go since my parents got me a Canon Rebel T6 for my 16th birthday. A lot of people have long hair, but most people recognize me with a camera around my neck. If it wasn't for the pictures that I've taken, many of the adventures that my friends and I have been on wouldn't be documented. We simply would've forgotten some of the small moments that can only be shared visually to get the full scope.

This website started as a fan project for the artist Ryan Caraveo, but it was soon forgotten about and abandoned. However, I'm back and will be using this as my personal site for whatever the **** I want it to be as Big Internet collapses on itself.

There's not much here yet, so I thank you so much for taking the time to read this!
